SECTION 5: SOWING INDEX ENGINE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Status: ✅ COMPLETE & TESTED

Implementation:
  ✅ SowingIndexEngine.kt - weighted algorithm

  ✅ Factors & weights:
     - Soil Moisture: 25%
     - Temperature: 20%
     - NPK (Nitrogen + Phosphorus + Potassium): 25%
     - Weather (humidity + rainfall): 20%
     - pH: 10%

  ✅ Crop-specific thresholds implemented:
     - SUGARCANE: Moisture 60-85%, Temp 18-35°C, pH 6.0-7.5
     - RAGI: Moisture 30-70%, Temp 15-32°C, pH 5.5-7.0
     - PADDY: Moisture 70-95%, Temp 18-35°C, pH 5.5-7.0
     - COTTON: Moisture 40-70%, Temp 20-35°C, pH 6.0-8.0
     - CORN: Moisture 50-80%, Temp 18-30°C, pH 5.8-7.0
     - + 7 more crops (Wheat, Soybean, Groundnut, Sunflower, Chili, Tomato, Onion)

  ✅ Status classification:
     - GREEN (≥ 0.70): "Excellent conditions! Optimal sowing window."
     - AMBER (≥ 0.50): "Borderline conditions. Consider waiting..."
     - RED (< 0.50): "Conditions not suitable for sowing."

  ✅ Real-time calculation: updates when data refreshes on Dashboard
  ✅ Visualization: animated ring chart with color change (green/amber/red)
